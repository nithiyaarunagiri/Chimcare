The Three Killers

What's Really Hiding In Your Chimney?

Most homeowners don't realize the silent hazards building up inside their kitchen exhaust — until it's too late.

Creosote Buildup

Fire Hazard #1

A tar-like substance that coats your chimney walls every time you cook. Highly flammable, and the leading cause of chimney fires.

Airborne Grease

Health Hazard #1

Microscopic oil particles that travel through your kitchen air and settle deep in your lungs. Invisible, odorless, and dangerous.

Blocked Airflow

Efficiency Killer #1

Soot and debris reduce suction power by up to 60%, forcing your fan to overwork and driving up energy bills every month.
