Most Asked

Common Questions, Straight Answers

These are the questions our customers ask us most often. Click any question to see the answer.

How often should I get my chimney cleaned?

For most homes, we recommend a professional chimney clean once a year — ideally before winter. If you use your fireplace frequently or burn wood often, twice a year is safer. Restaurants and commercial kitchens typically need their exhaust systems cleaned every 3 to 6 months depending on cooking volume.

How do I know if my chimney needs cleaning?

Warning signs include a strong smoky smell in the room, black soot falling into the fireplace, poor draft or smoke backing up, and a visible buildup of creosote on the flue walls. If it's been more than a year since your last clean, book a free inspection — we'll tell you exactly what needs doing.

Is chimney cleaning messy? Will it damage my home?

Not at all. Our technicians lay protective drop cloths, use industrial HEPA vacuums that capture 99.97% of fine dust, and clean up completely before leaving. Most customers say they can't even tell we were there — except for the cleaner air and better draft.

How long does a typical clean take?

A standard residential chimney sweep takes 1.5 to 2.5 hours. A full kitchen exhaust clean for a restaurant takes 4 to 6 hours. Duct cleaning varies from 2 to 5 hours depending on system size. We always confirm the estimated time when we send your written quote.

Do you charge extra for emergency or weekend calls?

Weekend and evening appointments are available at no additional charge for standard bookings. Emergency call-outs (same-day or after hours) carry a small surcharge, which we confirm upfront before dispatching a technician. No hidden fees — ever.

Are your cleaning products safe for pets and children?

Yes. We use only eco-friendly, non-toxic, biodegradable cleaning products. No harsh chemicals, no lingering fumes, no danger to pets, children, or family members with asthma. Safety is at the core of every method we use.

What happens if the problem comes back after cleaning?

Every job we do comes with a written satisfaction guarantee. If airflow drops again, smoke returns, or you notice any issue within the warranty period, we come back and fix it — free of charge. We stand behind every clean we deliver.

01 Do you clean commercial kitchen exhausts?

Yes. Commercial kitchen exhaust cleaning is one of our core services. We work with restaurants, cafes, hotels, and catering kitchens — following NFPA 96 standards and issuing compliance reports after every clean.

Absolutely. We use specialised rotary brushes and long-reach HEPA vacuum systems that clean the inside of your ducts through existing access points. No demolition, no mess, no wall damage.

Before You Call

Quick Troubleshooting Guide

Not sure if you need a professional clean? Check these common symptoms to see if it's time to book an inspection.

Smoke Won't Go Up the Chimney

If smoke rolls back into the room instead of going up the flue, your chimney is likely blocked by soot, a bird's nest, or creosote buildup.

  • Open a window to clear the smoke
  • Check if the damper is fully open
  • Book a professional flue inspection

Exhaust Fan Has Weak Suction

If your kitchen hood isn't pulling smoke and steam out effectively, the fan blades or ductwork are likely coated in thick grease.

  • Clean or replace the metal filters
  • Check for visible grease buildup
  • Schedule a deep duct clean

Loud Roaring Sound in Fireplace

A low, rumbling roar that sounds like a freight train is the classic sign of a chimney fire. This is a severe emergency.

  • Evacuate the house immediately
  • Call the fire department (000)
  • Do not use the fireplace again until inspected

Grease Dripping from the Hood

If liquid grease is dripping down your kitchen walls or from the exhaust hood, the system is dangerously overloaded.

  • Stop using the exhaust fan immediately
  • Place a bucket or towels under the drip
  • Call for an emergency deep clean
