Our Story

From A Small Family Business To A Trusted Name

ChimCare started in 2011 with one van, two brothers, and a simple belief: every kitchen deserves clean air. What began as a small chimney sweeping service quickly grew into a full-system kitchen exhaust cleaning company.

Today, we serve homes and restaurants across the city — but the values that started it all still drive every job we do.

Family-First Values

Every home we enter is treated with the same care as our own.

Certified & Insured

Every technician is licensed, background-checked, and fully insured.

Eco-Friendly Approach

Non-toxic products safe for children, pets, and the planet.

What Drives Us

Our Mission & Our Vision

Two guiding principles that shape every decision we make — from the products we use to the way we treat your home.

Our Mission

To protect every family and restaurant from the invisible dangers of grease, soot, and blocked airflow — through professional cleaning, honest advice, and a genuine commitment to health.

Our Vision

A world where every kitchen — home or commercial — has clean, safe, and properly maintained ventilation. Where fire hazards from grease buildup are a thing of the past, not a daily risk.
