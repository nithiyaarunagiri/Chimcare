Why It Matters

Skipping A Clean Isn't Just About Dirt

Grease, soot, and blocked airflow don't just look bad — they quietly turn your kitchen into a fire risk and your air into a health hazard. Here's what proper cleaning protects you from.

01

Fire Prevention

Grease and creosote are the leading cause of kitchen and chimney fires. Regular cleaning removes the fuel before it ignites.

02

Healthier Air

Microscopic grease particles and soot travel through your kitchen air and settle deep in your lungs. Clean air means healthier lungs.

03

Lower Energy Bills

Blocked ducts force your exhaust fan to work harder. A clean system uses less power and keeps your energy costs down.

04

Longer Appliance Life

Regular servicing prevents soot damage and blockages, extending the lifespan of your fan, motor, and entire exhaust system.

Our Specialised Techniques

Industry-Grade Methods, Home-Safe Results

We don't just brush and vacuum. Every service uses specialised equipment and technique — because a truly clean system needs more than surface work.

1

Rotary Power Brushing

Motorised rotary brushes scrub every inch of the flue — removing baked-on creosote that hand brushes can't reach.

2

Heated Pressure Wash

Hot-water pressure cleaning dissolves stubborn grease and grime deep inside ductwork — without damaging the metal.

3

HEPA Vacuum Extraction

Industrial HEPA vacuums capture 99.97% of fine soot and dust — no mess, no particles released back into your kitchen.

4

Camera Verification

Internal cameras confirm every duct is fully clear — before and after — so you see the real results, not just our word.
